Before you book

A few cake details.

Can you create a design around my theme?

Share your theme, colour scheme or inspiration pictures so we can discuss a design. Final decoration and any personalisation are agreed in your quote.

How much will my cake cost?

Each cake is quoted individually based on size, design and decoration. Include your approximate budget and serving numbers when you enquire.

How far ahead should I enquire?

Contact us as early as you can with your date. Availability and any booking requirements will be confirmed before you place your order.

Can you cater for allergies?

Please discuss allergies and dietary requirements before ordering. We’ll confirm ingredients and suitability for your chosen cake; don’t assume a design is suitable without checking.
