Questions from workplace buyers

The practical bits.

Can we arrange regular workplace deliveries?

Yes, get in touch to discuss a recurring arrangement. We’ll agree quantities, frequency and delivery details with you before confirming. For catering counters, start with an order size that fits your expected sales and review demand together.

What about allergies and dietary requirements?

Please ask for the ingredients and allergen information for your chosen bakes before ordering. Don’t assume a product is suitable for a particular allergy or dietary requirement.

Where do you deliver?

The delivery area is being finalised. Include your location in your enquiry so the sisters can confirm availability.

Does the employer pay, or do employees buy the bakes?

Either model can be discussed. For office treat boxes, the employer buys the order for staff to enjoy. For wholesale supply, your café or catering operator buys the stock and handles employee sales through its own system. Prices and order terms are agreed before confirmation.

Are there minimum orders or fixed delivery days?

Minimum quantities, delivery availability, payment terms and any arrangements for unsold stock must be agreed as part of your quote. Tell us your location and expected demand so we can discuss a suitable arrangement.
